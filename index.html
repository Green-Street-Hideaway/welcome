Find us

In the heart of the
Savinja Valley

We're tucked away on a quiet green street in Šempeter v Savinjski Dolini — a charming town in the Lower Savinja Valley, surrounded by orchards, forests, and the gentle Savinja River.

Perfectly placed for day trips: ancient Roman history, thermal spas, the mystical Hell Cave (Jama Pekel), medieval castles and the famous Beer Fountain of Žalec are all within easy reach.

Šempeter sits in the heart of Slovenia's hop-growing region, so flat, easy cycling routes through the "Green Gold" hop fields and along the Savinja River start right at the door — with rewarding climbs in the surrounding hills if you want them.

Good to know

Questions guests often ask

A few practical details before you book. Anything else — just message us, we're happy to help.

How do I check availability and book?
Everything — live availability, current rates for your dates, and secure booking — is on our Booking.com listing. It is always kept up to date, so it is the fastest and most reliable way to reserve your stay.
How many guests can stay?
The apartment comfortably sleeps 2 to 5 guests. There are two bedrooms plus a sofa bed in the living area, which makes it a great fit for a family or a small group of friends.
Do I get the whole apartment to myself?
Yes. You have the entire ground-floor apartment — 2 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, kitchen and living area — plus your own private garden and terrace with a BBQ. Nothing is shared with other guests.
What time is check-in and check-out?
Check-in is between 14:00 and 21:00, and check-out is until 11:30. If your travel plans fall outside those hours, message us — we will do our best to help.
How does check-in work?
It is keyless, so there are no keys to collect and nobody to wait for. You will receive your own personal access code on the day of arrival, together with simple entry instructions. We are always reachable by phone or WhatsApp if you would rather be met in person.
Where do I park, and how do I find the entrance?
A dedicated space in the courtyard is reserved for you, marked with a "Green Street Parking" sign — free of charge. From there it is a 20 m walk along the left-hand side of the house; your private entrance is on the right.
Are there shops nearby?
Yes, several within walking distance: Mercator is 550 m away, with Spar and the Fijavž bakery both around 850 m. For a bigger shop, Hofer (3.3 km), Lidl (3.6 km) and Tuš (4.2 km) are a short drive away in Žalec.
What is provided in the apartment?
Fresh bed linen and towels, a fully equipped kitchen (hob, oven, dishwasher and coffee machine), high-speed Wi-Fi, a Smart TV with streaming apps, and access to the fitness room. We also leave you basic spices, tea and extra coffee capsules, toiletries such as shampoo, shower gel, tissues and spare toilet paper, and a universal adapter for international guests. Just bring your bags.
How do I get there from the airport?
Ljubljana Airport (LJU) is about 63 km away — roughly a 50-minute drive. Celje train station is 14 km away, with direct connections to Ljubljana. A car is the easiest way to explore the valley, and parking here is free.
What is there to do nearby?
Plenty. The Roman Necropolis is within walking distance, the Beer Fountain in Žalec is 4 km away, and three thermal spas — Terme Dobrna, Thermana Laško and Rimske Terme — are all within about 35 minutes by car. The mystical Hell Cave (Jama Pekel), flower parks and medieval castles make easy day trips. Šempeter is also the heart of Slovenia's hop-growing region, so flat cycling routes through the "Green Gold" hop fields and along the Savinja River start right on the doorstep.
